> ## 3. Unrecorded architectural decisions
> - The migration effectively decides that enum validation is application-only, not SQLite-enforced, by dropping the spec’s `CHECK` constraints from `node`, `edge`, `obligation`, `lease`, `trace_event`, and `row_audit`. This is load-bearing because it weakens the substrate contract and changes invalid-data failure modes from DB-time to app-time. No ADR currently records this; ADR-0015 says the initial migration should carry the tech-spec §4 checks from day 1.
> - The migration comments and DDL effectively decide that phase-2 bootstrap may execute `vec0` virtual-table creation before `sqlite-vec` is wired. This is load-bearing because it changes the DB-init contract for every fresh database from “tolerate missing sqlite-vec until phase 5” to “module must be available during migrate.” No ADR currently records that requirement; ADR-0015 records the opposite tolerance story.
